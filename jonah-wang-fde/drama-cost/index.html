Best practice

The biggest saving comes from making more video generations usable.

−30%at 75% success

To cut the drama's cost, raise the quality and success rate of video generation

Video is about 90% of the cost, and every failed take is paid for ($3.89 with its prompt) and then generated again. Raising the success rate saves far more than cutting images or prompts.

50% success · $1,029 today75% · $718 (−$311)100% · $563 (−$466)
  • Lock assets first: fixed character sheets and locations keep faces and sets from drifting between shots, a common reason takes are rejected.
  • Fix problems at the storyboard: a storyboard image costs $0.10 and a video take $3.89, about 39× more, so approve every keyframe before generating video from it.
  • Generate video from approved keyframes (I2V) and chain the last frame of one shot into the next, so motion starts from a known good image.

Seedance 2.0 feature

Multi-Subject Reference

Seedance 2.0 takes several reference images at once: multi-angle views of a subject (front, side, back) and multi-image references such as scene layouts and storyboards. Characters and staging stay the same from shot to shot, which raises the video success rate.

  • If the images must be used in a set order, upload them in that order.
  • In the prompt, refer to them as "Image 1", "Image 2" … "Image n".
  • Prompt template:
Reference/extract the [subject] in [Image n] to generate [scene description], keeping the characteristics of the [subject] consistent.
